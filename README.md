# My Favorite Series: Owari no Seraph

## About
**Owari no Seraph** (*Seraph of the End*) is a dark fantasy anime set in a world where a mysterious virus wipes out almost everyone over the age of 13, and vampires take over the world. *Yuichiro Hyakuya* vows to destroy them all.

> A story about family, loyalty, and what you're willing to sacrifice for the people you love.

---

## Why I Love It
1. The story is intense and full of twists
2. The scenes in the anime were breathtaking
3. The action scenes and soundtrack are amazing, especially during the Salt King
